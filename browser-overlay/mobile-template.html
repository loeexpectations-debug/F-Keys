F Keys

Add a floating keypad with F1–F12 and Enter to your webpage in Chrome.

Set up the bookmark

  1. Tap Copy bookmark address below.
  2. In Chrome, bookmark any regular webpage. Edit that bookmark and name it F Keys Pad.
  3. Replace its entire address with what you copied, then save it.

Use it on your webpage

Open your webpage. Type F Keys Pad in Chrome’s address bar and tap the matching bookmark suggestion. Tap the field you want to use, then tap a key. Launch the bookmark again after a full page load.
