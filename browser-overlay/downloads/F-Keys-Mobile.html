F Keys

Add a floating keypad with F1–F12 and Enter to your webpage in Chrome.

Set up once on your phone

  1. Tap Copy bookmark address below.
  2. In Chrome, bookmark any regular webpage. Edit that bookmark and name it F Keys Pad.
  3. Replace its entire address with what you copied. The last six characters should be /*FK*/. Save the bookmark.

Use it on your webpage

Open your webpage. Type F Keys Pad in Chrome’s address bar and tap the matching bookmark suggestion. Once the toolbar appears, tap an F-key or Enter. For an embedded terminal, tap the terminal area once to direct the keys there. Minimize folds the toolbar into a small F Keys tab. Launch the bookmark again after a full page load.

Try a screen without a text box

Tap a key to see which one this page received.
